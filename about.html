About Gravity Games

The Gravity Games Mission

At Gravity Games (gravitygamez.fun), our mission is to resurrect the magic of instant-play browser gaming with modern web technologies. We believe gaming should be instantaneous, zero-friction, and completely open to anyone with a web browser.

No 50GB downloads, no bloated app store installs, no forced account creations, and no intrusive permission prompts. You click, wait a moment, and you are immediately inside the game.

Engineering Excellence & Architecture

Gravity Games is engineered with strict performance budgets:

• Zero Bloat: Built using pure semantic HTML5, modern CSS3 custom properties, and Vanilla JavaScript (ES6+) for instant loading on both mobile and desktop.
• Hardware Accelerated 60 FPS: Every game leverages native WebGL, HTML5 2D Canvas pipelines, and procedural Web Audio synthesis.
• Mobile-First Controls: Responsive stages, touch-friendly controls, and instant fullscreen support across phones, tablets, and desktops.
• Respect for Privacy: Zero-Auth architecture utilizing local client-side localStorage for favorites and recently played games.

Our Game Library

From high-stakes rage platformers like Don't Trust The Floor and One Button Hell, to first-person 3D tunnel racers like Prism Rush, tactical board games like Obsidian Chess, and open-world sandboxes like TerraVox 3D Sandbox and Savannah Sovereign, our handcrafted catalog offers deep, varied mechanics for every player.
