Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 26, 2026 · Last Updated: November 2026

1. Introduction & Overview

Welcome to Gravity Games (accessible via gravitygamez.fun). We are committed to protecting your privacy and providing a safe, enjoyable gaming experience. This Privacy Policy outlines our practices regarding data collection, client-side storage technologies, and third-party advertising partners.

2. Zero-Auth Architecture & Client-Side localStorage

Gravity Games operates on a Zero-Authentication (No-Login) Architecture. We do not require, prompt for, or store user accounts, passwords, emails, or personal identities to play any game on our portal.

To provide core convenience features (such as tracking your Recently Played Games, storing your Bookmarked Favorites, and remembering your Theater Mode preferences), we utilize your browser's local HTML5 storage (localStorage). This data resides exclusively on your local device and is never transmitted to, harvested by, or sold on any external database.

3. Third-Party Advertising & Google AdSense Compliance

We partner with third-party advertising networks, including Google AdSense, to serve advertisements when you visit our website. These advertising companies may use cookies and web beacons to serve ads based on your prior visits to our website or other sites on the Internet:

• Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to our site and/or other sites on the Internet.
• Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or by visiting aboutads.info.

4. Log Files & Anonymous Diagnostics

Like most modern web platforms, our hosting servers automatically log standard anonymous diagnostic requests. These logs may include IP addresses, browser user-agent strings, referral pages, and timestamps for DDoS mitigation, system health diagnostics, and edge caching optimization.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Gravity Games is designed for general audiences and complies strictly with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). We do not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13.

6. Contacting Us Regarding Privacy

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy or data security on Gravity Games, you may contact our engineering team via our Contact Page or via email at privacy@gravitygamez.fun.
