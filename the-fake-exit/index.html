How to Play

Controls

Move with A / D or Left / Right Arrows. Jump with Space, W, or Up Arrow. Hold Jump longer for higher arcs. Press R to instant-restart and ESC to pause.

The Game Lies

Floors may crumble or vanish. Exits may run away, teleport you, or turn out to be traps. Watch closely—every trick has a learnable pattern.

Purple Objects

Purple bounce pads launch you upward. Purple pads flip gravity. Purple keys unlock sealed exits or transform the room.

Survival Tip

Nothing is random. If a trap surprises you once, bait it out on your next run. Stay patient across all 40 levels.
